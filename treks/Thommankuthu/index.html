About the Trek

Idukki's Enchanted
Seven-Tier Water Wonderland

Tucked within the deep emerald foothills of the Kaliyar Forest Range in Idukki district, Thommankuthu is a breathtaking geological spectacle where the mountain river Kannadiyaru cascades over seven majestic rock terraces.

The trek is steeped in local folklore, named in tribute to Thomman, a legendary local jungle hunter who was swept over the roaring falls in the late 19th century. Today, under the sustainable guardianship of the Kerala Forest Department and Bootpaths certified leaders, the sanctuary stands as one of South India's premier eco-tourism trails.

As you hike along the river gorge, you'll cross an iconic hanging suspension bridge hovering over rushing rapids, explore mysterious prehistoric granite caves like Nakkayam and Chekuthan Kuthu once inhabited by early tribal forest dwellers, and dip into serene crystal-clear natural bathing pools carved into ancient black granite.

Stage 01

Ecotourism Base to Hanging Bridge & Lower Falls

08:30 AM – 10:30 AM • Forest Checkpost to Steps 1 & 2
↗ +120m Gain 2.5 km Trail
Phase 1 • River Rapids & Suspension Bridge

The expedition begins at the Thommankuthu Forest Ecotourism Centre in Kaliyar Range. Trekkers assemble for orientation, permits audit, and Forest Department safety briefings.

The trail opens along the picturesque Kannadiyaru riverbank. Within 600 meters, you reach the iconic timber-plank suspension hanging bridge swaying gently over rushing white water rapids. Continuing along the rocky trail beneath colossal teak trees and bamboo groves brings you to the first two roaring waterfall tiers with swirling granite plunge pools.

08:30 AM
Assembly & Permit Registration

Meet Bootpaths leaders and licensed Forest Department indigenous guides at the reception centre. Safety briefing and hydration check.

09:15 AM
Suspension Bridge River Crossing

Cross the wooden hanging bridge over the rushing Kannadiyaru gorge. Magnificent vantage point for photography and mist inhalation.

10:00 AM
Lower Waterfall Steps (Tiers 1 & 2)

Arrive at the first two cascade terraces where water thunders into deep turquoise granite basins surrounded by lush ferns.

Stage 02

Ezhunilakuthu Cascades to Prehistoric Caves

10:30 AM – 01:30 PM • Steps 3–5 & Nakkayam Cave Shelters
↗ +190m Gain 4.2 km Trail
Phase 2 • Ancient Rock Shelters & Mid Cascades

Venturing beyond the casual tourist perimeter, the trail transforms into a deep tropical evergreen rainforest corridor requiring moderate stamina and foot placement.

You traverse the middle tiers of Ezhunilakuthu waterfall where water tumbles down staircases of polished gneiss and granite. Here, concealed behind thick curtains of hanging ficus roots and moss, lie the ancient prehistoric caves of Nakkayam and Chekuthan Kuthu, historically utilized as shelters by ancient forest inhabitants.

10:30 AM
Middle Cascades Ascent (Steps 3 & 4)

11:45 AM

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for the Thommankuthu Seven Steps Trek

What is the difficulty level of the Thommankuthu 7-Steps trek?

+

The full circuit is rated Easy to Moderate. The initial 1.5 km to the hanging bridge and 1st step waterfall is on gentle, well-cleared paths suitable for all age groups. Advancing to the higher tiers (steps 3 to 7) and the prehistoric caves involves moderate climbs over rocky granite outcrops, natural roots, and riverbank trails spanning approximately 10–12 km round-trip.

Can we swim or bathe in the waterfalls?

+

Yes, bathing is permitted exclusively in the designated shallow natural granite pools under the supervision of Forest Department guides. Bathing is strictly prohibited near the deep gorges, whirlpools, and sudden waterfall drops for safety reasons.

What is the best season to experience Thommankuthu?

+

The prime window is from September to May. During post-monsoon months (September to December), the waterfalls roar in their fullest glory while the trails are vibrant green. In winter and early summer (January to May), water currents are gentle, clear, and ideal for swimming and cave trekking.

Is prior forest permission mandatory for the deep trek?

+

Yes. While tourists can access the first few cascades with basic entry, deeper treks to the 7 tiers and prehistoric caves require official eco-tourism permits and certified guides from the Kerala Forest Department (Kaliyar Range). When you book with Bootpaths, all permits, guides, and safety clearances are arranged in advance.

How do we reach the Thommankuthu base camp?

+

Thommankuthu is situated approximately 18 km from Thodupuzha town in Idukki district. It is well-connected by road from Kochi / Ernakulam (approx. 65 km) and Kottayam (approx. 60 km). Bootpaths can also arrange coordinated cab transfers from Kochi Airport or Ernakulam Railway Station upon request.
