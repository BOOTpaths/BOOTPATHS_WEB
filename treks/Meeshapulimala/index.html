About the Trek

The Cloud Kingdom &
The Mist of Charlie

Standing tall at 2,640 metres (8,661 feet) in the pristine High Ranges of Munnar, Meeshapulimala is the second highest peak in the Western Ghats and the highest summit in South India accessible to passionate trekkers.

The mountain derives its name from its distinctive profile: "Meesha" (whiskers), "Puli" (tiger), and "Mala" (mountain) — a dramatic ridgeline of eight undulating peaks resembling the face and whiskers of a roaring tiger rising out of misty Shola valleys.

Immortalized across India by the cult Malayalam cinema Charlie — "Meeshapulimalayil manju veezhunathu kandittundo?" — this trek is a transcendental journey. You will travel by rugged 4x4 Jeep through emerald tea gardens to Rhodo Valley, camp under a blanket of unpolluted stars, and summit at dawn as golden sun rays illuminate an endless ocean of fluffy white clouds beneath your feet.

Detailed Day-by-Day Walkthrough

In-depth hour-by-hour breakdown, trail terrain, meals, wildlife, and photo guides

Day 1

Munnar Arrival • 4x4 Safari • Rhodo Valley Alpine Camp

⏱️
Duration: 4–5 hours (Jeep safari + acclimatization stroll)
📈
Altitude Gain: 1,850 m → 2,160 m (+310m vertical gain)
🥾
Difficulty & Terrain: Moderate off-road dirt trail & shola forest walk
🌟
Trail Highlights: Munnar tea estates, KFDC forest checkpost, flowering Rhododendrons, campfire dinner under stars

10:30 AM – Registration & Briefing: Arrive at the Kerala Forest Development Corporation (KFDC) basecamp in Munnar. Complete registration permits, meet your certified Bootpaths mountain leaders, and review medical & safety protocols.

12:00 PM – Rugged 4x4 Off-Road Mountain Safari: Board 4x4 Jeeps for a thrilling 24 km mountain climb passing lush tea plantations, eucalyptus slopes, and high-altitude mist gates up to Rhodo Valley.

02:30 PM – Campsite Check-in: Settle into high-altitude alpine dome tents equipped with thermal sleeping bags at Rhodo Valley (2,160m) — South India's highest motorable basecamp.

04:30 PM – Acclimatization Walk: Take a tranquil walk along the crystal mountain stream and flowering wild Rhododendron arboreum grove. Keep watch for the Nilgiri Tahr on rocky crags.

07:30 PM – Campfire & Traditional Kerala Dinner: Gather around a crackling campfire in the crisp mountain air. Relish freshly prepared Kerala meals followed by a briefing on tomorrow's dawn summit assault.

Day 2

Dawn Summit Ascent (2,640m) • Sea of Clouds • Return Journey

⏱️
Duration: 5–6 hours total (8 km summit hike + return safari)
📈
Altitude Gain/Loss: 2,160 m → 2,640 m (Summit) → 1,850 m (Basecamp)
🥾
Difficulty & Terrain: Moderate ridge trail, rocky outcrops & alpine grasslands
🌟
Trail Highlights: Pandava Cave, 8-peak tiger-whisker ridge walk, sunrise over the sea of clouds, 360° Western Ghats views

04:30 AM – Dawn Awakening: Wake to the crisp mountain breeze and hot chai. Equip headlamps and warm layers for the summit push.

05:00 AM – Summit Assault Commences: Trek under a canopy of stars past the mystical boulder overhang of Pandava Cave (2,300m), entering rolling montane grasslands.

06:00 AM – The 8-Peak Tiger Whisker Ridge: As the horizon turns purple and gold, traverse the 8 undulating peaks with swirling mist rising from the Tamil Nadu valleys.

06:45 AM – Crowning Meeshapulimala Summit (2,640m): Stand on the rocky summit of South India's highest trekkable mountain! Witness the spectacular sunrise over an endless sea of white clouds — the world-famous "Mist of Charlie".

08:30 AM – Descent & Hot Breakfast: Hike back down to Rhodo Valley for hot South Indian breakfast (Idlis, Vadas, Upma, Coffee).

11:30 AM – 4x4 Jeep Descent to Basecamp: Board the 4x4 Jeeps back to KFDC Basecamp. Conclude an unforgettable mountain journey.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Meeshapulimala expedition

How difficult is the Meeshapulimala Trek? Can beginners join?

▼

The trek is rated Moderate. Since the rugged 4x4 Jeep takes you all the way up to Rhodo Valley at 2,160m, the walking trek is approximately 8 km round-trip across the 8 rolling peaks to the summit at 2,640m. Any individual with reasonable cardio fitness, comfortable walking stamina, and sturdy footwear can easily complete this trek.

What is the best time of the year to witness the "Mist of Charlie"?

▼

The prime season spans from September to May. September to November (Post-Monsoon) offers emerald green grasslands and raging waterfalls. December to February (Winter) delivers the most iconic misty mornings, sea of clouds, and crisp temperatures dropping to near freezing. March to April brings blooming red Rhododendron arboreum flowers in Rhodo Valley.

Are permits mandatory, and can we trek without official guides?

▼

No solo or unpermitted trekking is allowed. Meeshapulimala is an ecologically protected forest reserve managed strictly by the Kerala Forest Development Corporation (KFDC). Bootpaths secures all mandatory forest entry permits, tent bookings, and authorized tribal guides for every group.

What are the accommodation and bathroom facilities at Rhodo Valley?

▼

You will stay in sturdy alpine dome tents pitched on flat grasslands with thermal sleeping bags and pillows. Well-maintained western/indian shared toilets and washrooms are available at the KFDC campsite facility. Note that being a high-altitude wilderness camp, electricity is solar-powered with limited charging points.

How cold does it get at night and during the dawn summit?

▼

During the day, pleasant temperatures range between 15°C and 22°C. At night and during the 5:00 AM dawn summit climb, temperatures regularly hover between 4°C and 10°C, and occasionally drop near 0°C with ground frost in mid-winter. A warm thermal layer, fleece/down jacket, beanie, and gloves are highly recommended.

How do I reach the starting basecamp in Munnar?

▼

The nearest airport and major railway junction is Cochin International Airport (COK) / Aluva Railway Station, located about 115 km (3.5 hours drive) from Munnar town. Frequent KSRTC buses and private cabs operate between Kochi/Ernakulam and Munnar. Our team can also arrange private pickup transfers on request.
