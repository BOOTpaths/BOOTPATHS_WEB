About the Trail

Kumbakarai to Vattakanal:
The Ancient Foothill Highway

The trek starts at Kumbakarai Falls, about 9 km from Periyakulam. A steep, rocky climb takes you out of the foothills and into a forested valley. As you approach Vellagavi, small farm plots appear between stretches of forest, with coffee, cardamom, and avocado grown around the village. This is the longest climbing day, so settle into a steady pace early.

After a night near Vellagavi, the trail climbs again towards Vattakanal. The upper section passes through shola forest and eucalyptus groves near Dolphin's Nose, a rock viewpoint overlooking the Palani Hills. From there, the final stretch climbs past exposed tree roots and local shops to the road in Vattakanal.

Detailed Day-by-Day Schedule

Exact timestamps, elevation profiles, meals, and terrain details

DAY 1

Kumbakarai Falls to Vellagavi

Meet at the foothills and embark on the longest climbing segment of the trek, ascending into lush agro-forest valleys before camping under the stars near Vellagavi.

8:00 AM

Meet at the Kumbakarai Falls reception point after breakfast. Meet the local guide, complete entry formalities, and fill your bottles before setting off.

8:30 AM

Start the steep climb from the foothills. The opening stretch is rocky before entering the forested valley.

Midday

Break for the included lunch along the route or at camp, depending on group pace.

3:00 PM

Reach the Vellagavi area and settle in at the campsite. Walk through the village and observe its barefoot custom if time and local guidance allow.

7:30 PM

Dinner and overnight stay at the campsite near Vellagavi.

DAY 2

Vellagavi to Vattakanal via Dolphin's Nose

Climb through shola groves, witness stunning morning vistas over the Palani Hills, visit Dolphin's Nose, and top out at Vattakanal.

8:00 AM

Have the included breakfast at camp, pack up, and start the climb towards Vattakanal.

Late Morning

Pass through shola and eucalyptus stretches towards Dolphin's Nose. Stop at the viewpoint if the guide confirms access and conditions.

12:30 PM

Complete the final climb to the road in Vattakanal. The BOOTpaths trek ends here; arrange onward journey separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Vellagavi trek

Is this suitable for beginners?

▼

Best suited to trekkers already comfortable with several hours of sustained climbing on uneven ground; Day 1 is the longer climb and Day 2 still has steep sections.

How do I reach the start, and where do I arrange pickup?

▼

Meet at Kumbakarai Falls reception point (~9 km from Periyakulam, reachable by auto/taxi); arrange onward pickup at Vattakanal near Kodaikanal.

Do I have to walk barefoot throughout the trek?

▼

Wear trekking shoes on the forest trail; inside Vellagavi, follow the guide's instructions on where to remove footwear out of respect for village custom.

Can I refill water on the Day 1 climb?

▼

Start with enough water for the climb to camp as lower-section refill points can be unreliable.

Can we bathe at Kumbakarai Falls?

▼

The falls are the starting landmark; a dip is outside the planned itinerary and depends on local access and water conditions.

What should I pack for the overnight stay?

▼

Grippy trekking shoes, small backpack, reusable water bottles, rain protection, warm layer for camp, headlamp, personal medication, government-issued photo ID, and a pouch to carry wrappers back out.
