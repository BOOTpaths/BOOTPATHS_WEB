About the Trek

Kerala's Premier
Amphibious Eco-Adventure

Chimmini Wildlife Sanctuary is a pristine ecological haven in Thrissur district where the rugged, mist-wrapped crests of the Western Ghats cradle a magnificent 10-square-kilometer emerald water reservoir.

What makes the Chimmini Amphi Trek uniquely captivating is its seamless amphibious rhythm: an exhilarating blend of an 8 km guided overland wilderness trek through moist deciduous and tropical evergreen jungle, followed by an unforgettable circular bamboo coracle boat (locally known as Kottavanji) voyage across mirror-smooth reservoir channels.

Accompanied by Kerala Forest Department certified indigenous guides and Bootpaths trek leads, you will discover hidden forest streams, navigate wild bamboo canopies, and observe diverse wildlife — from the flamboyant Indian Malabar Giant Squirrel and endangered hornbills to herds of sambar deer and Asian elephants congregating at the lake edge.

Morning Assembly, Dam Panorama & Deep Evergreen Forest Entry

Assemble at the Chimmini Forest Eco-Tourism Centre in Echippara at 08:30 AM. Meet your certified Bootpaths trek lead and registered tribal naturalist guides. Following Forest Department registration and a safety briefing, begin the walk across the masonry crest of Chimmini Dam.

Take in spectacular morning panoramas of the 10-square-kilometer reservoir surrounded by emerald peaks of the Western Ghats. Transition into the core jungle trail through towering rosewood, terminalia, and wild bamboo thickets.

Listen to the distinctive morning symphony of the Malabar Whistling Thrush and watch for the multi-hued Malabar Giant Squirrel foraging in the high canopy before arriving at the Virakuthodu stream crossing.

Authentic Bamboo Kottavanji Coracle Expedition & Reservoir Backwaters

Equipped with standardized safety lifejackets, step into the round woven bamboo coracle boats (kottavanji) for the definitive "Amphi" water experience. Guided by experienced indigenous boatmen, glide serenely across the calm lake surface.

Navigate through secluded channels between submerged forest islands, observing kingfishers, cormorants, and darter birds drying their wings on protruding tree snags. Enjoy the rare sensation of circular drifting on pristine mountain waters.

Return to the Echippara main jetty by late afternoon for evening Kerala tea, banana fritters/snacks, debriefing, certificate presentation, and warm farewells by 04:30 PM.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trekking and coracle boating in Chimmini Wildlife Sanctuary

Is prior booking required for the Chimmini Amphi Trek?

+

Yes. Because Chimmini Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected reserve governed by the Kerala Forest and Wildlife Department, daily entries are regulated to conserve wildlife habitats. Bootpaths manages all official permits, eco-development committee (EDC) clearances, certified tribal guides, and coracle slot reservations in advance.

What does the "Amphi" in Chimmini Amphi Trek stand for?

+

"Amphi" signifies the unique amphibious nature of the journey — an immersive eco-adventure that covers both land and water. Trekkers traverse an 8 km trail through lush tropical evergreen forests and cross mountain streams, followed by an exhilarating circular bamboo coracle boat (Kottavanji) ride across the calm expanse of Chimmini reservoir.

Can beginners, families, and children join this trek?

+

Absolutely! The Chimmini Amphi Trek is classified as Easy to Moderate. The trail features gentle undulations through shaded forest paths and stream beds. It is popular with nature enthusiasts, photographers, families, and first-time trekkers. Children above 7 years who enjoy walking in nature can comfortably participate.

Is the coracle (Kottavanji) ride safe and are lifejackets provided?

+

Safety is our top priority. All coracle rides are steered by licensed local oarsmen who have navigated these waters for decades. Every participant is required to wear high-grade, certified personal flotation devices (lifejackets) throughout the lake journey. The reservoir waters are tranquil and calm.

How do I reach the Chimmini basecamp at Echippara?

+

The sanctuary base is at Echippara, approximately 40 km from Thrissur city, Kerala. The nearest major railway station is Thrissur (TCR - 40 km), and the nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK - 75 km). Well-paved roads connect via Varandarappilly. Bootpaths can also organize shared or dedicated private vehicle transfers upon request.

What ecological and plastic guidelines must be followed?

+

Chimmini Wildlife Sanctuary strictly enforces a zero-tolerance policy against plastic littering, smoking, alcohol, and disruptive sound devices. Single-use plastic bottles are prohibited. Trekkers are provided with filtered fresh drinking water refills and must carry reusable water bottles or flasks.
