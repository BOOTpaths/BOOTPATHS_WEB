Ring in 2027 Above the Clouds:

Schedule & Guide

2-Day New Year Itinerary

Curated timeline for 31 December 2026 – 1 January 2027

🗺️

Detailed Timeline & Route

From Suryanelli off-road ascent to the Bose Peak sunrise

DAY 1

Thursday, 31 December 2026 (Into the Mountains)

Meet at Suryanelli, take rugged 4x4 mountain jeeps up to Kolukkumalai, settle into camp, hike for sunset, and celebrate New Year's Eve around the campfire.

2:00 PM

Meet the BOOTpaths team at Suryanelli.

2:00–3:00 PM

Board off-road jeeps for the drive to Kolukkumalai.

3:00 PM

Arrive at the campsite, check in to your tent, and settle in.

5:00 PM

Head out for a guided sunset walk in the Jaguar Rock hills.

Evening

Return to camp for tea and refreshments.

7:30 PM

Have dinner at the campsite.

8:30 PM

Gather around the campfire, share stories, and welcome 2027 together.

DAY 2

Friday, 1 January 2027 (The First Sunrise)

Pre-dawn hike to Bose Peak for first light over the cloud ocean, followed by breakfast, an orthodox tea factory tour, and descent to Suryanelli.

4:30 AM

Begin the guided pre-dawn trek to Bose Peak.

Sunrise

Pause at the viewpoint for the first light of 2027 over the surrounding valleys and tea estates.

8:00 AM

Return to the campsite for morning tea and breakfast.

9:30 AM

Visit the Kolukkumalai Organic Tea Factory.

11:00 AM

Board the jeeps for the return drive.

12:00 Noon

Arrive at Suryanelli, where the trip concludes.

Note: Itinerary timings are approximate. Sunset, sunrise and stargazing depend on weather and visibility.
