About the Trek

Where Clouds Rest Below Your Feet:
The Crown of the Western Ghats

Kolukkumala is not merely a mountain viewpoint — it is an ethereal kingdom perched on the dramatic razorback boundary between Kerala's high ranges and the precipitous plains of Tamil Nadu.

Sitting at an altitude of 2,170 meters (7,130 feet), Kolukkumala proudly holds the global title of the highest organic tea plantation in the world. Established in 1935 by pioneering British planters, the tea estate continues to craft exquisite, highly prized orthodox tea using pure artisan methods passed down through four generations.

What transforms the Bootpaths expedition into an unforgettable pilgrimage is the pre-dawn ascent: embarking from Suryanelli base camp in rugged 4x4 off-road jeeps, conquering 10 km of boulder-strewn hairpin trails under starry skies, before reaching the eastern summit rim just as the sun rises over an endless, rolling Sea of Clouds (Kottagudi Cloud Bed). Hike along the sheer ridge to Singapara (Tiger Face Rock), peer over 4,000-foot drops, and explore the historic wood-fired tea factory where time has stood still since 1935.

Need Answers?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the sunrise timing, 4x4 jeep safari, fitness, and booking rules

Can beginners, children, and elderly travelers join the Kolukkumala trek?

Yes! Because 4x4 mountain jeeps cover the major 10 km steep ascent from Suryanelli base camp to the upper factory saddle, the actual walking portion is a moderate 3.5 km ridge stroll. Travelers with normal fitness, children above 7 years, and active seniors can comfortably participate. However, due to the bumpy off-road trail, pregnant women and persons with severe spinal disorders are advised against the jeep ride.

What is the best time of year to see the sea-of-clouds sunrise?

The legendary sea of clouds (Kottagudi cloud blanket) is most prominent from September to May. During post-monsoon and winter months (October through February), the temperature inversion creates thick, fluffy cloud beds almost every clear morning. The golden dawn breaks between 06:00 AM and 06:20 AM.

Where is the starting base camp and how do we reach there?

The expedition commences at Suryanelli Base Camp, located approximately 25 km from Munnar town center. You can drive your own vehicle to Suryanelli (safe paid parking available) or take a morning cab. Bootpaths also arranges private transfers from Munnar, Kochi, or Madurai upon advance request.

How rough is the 4x4 mountain jeep safari?

The 10 km jeep route is one of the most famous off-road tracks in South India. It is rocky, unpaved, and negotiates steep hairpin curves across boulders. Our fleet consists exclusively of well-maintained 4x4 Mahindra jeeps operated by licensed native mountain drivers who know every turn and rock. It is an adventurous, thrilling ride!

Can we purchase fresh tea directly from the 1935 Orthodox Factory?

Absolutely! The estate maintains an official sales counter where you can buy genuine, factory-fresh Kolukkumala orthodox black tea (BOP, Broken Orange Pekoe), Golden Flowery Pekoe, and organic Green Tea. These leaves are hand-packaged on site and make exceptional souvenirs.

What happens if weather is foggy or rainy?

Mountain weather is inherently dynamic. Even on overcast days, wind currents frequently clear the valley in bursts, creating dramatic misty vistas. Bootpaths leads monitor satellite weather predictions daily. In the rare event of extreme thunderstorms or forest department trail closures, we offer flexible rescheduling or full refunds.
