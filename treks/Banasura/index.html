About the Trek

The Conical Giant
of the Western Ghats

Rising abruptly from the lush valleys of Wayanad, Banasura Hill (2,073m) is the second highest mountain in the district and one of the most dramatically profiled peaks in South India. Named after the mythical Asura king Banasura, who according to folklore performed intense penance atop this summit, this mountain commands pure wilderness respect.

Unlike ordinary plateau treks, the Banasura trail is an authentic mountain ridge expedition. You will step off the edge of spice plantations, wade across crystal mountain brooks, navigate through dense tropical evergreen rainforest canopy, and burst into vast, windswept high-altitude Shola grasslands that ripple like green velvet beneath your feet.

The crown jewel of the trek is the final conical pyramid climb and the spine-tingling knife-edge ridge traverse. At 2,073 metres, the summit rewards you with an awe-inspiring 360-degree panorama: the sprawling Banasura Sagar Dam — the largest earth dam in India — sparkling with its dozens of submerged green islands, framed by the distant Brahmagiri and Chembra ranges.

Detailed Day-by-Day Route Guide

In-depth timeline, trail telemetry, meal schedules, and eco-guidelines

12:00 PM – 02:00 PM

Check-in at Banasura Foothill Camp & Traditional Feast

▾

Arrive at our scenic foothill base camp in Padinjarathara overlooking the distant misty Banasura contours. Trekkers are welcomed with refreshing spiced ginger-mint tea and treated to an authentic Kerala Sadya/buffet lunch prepared with fresh local mountain produce. Settle into your luxury dome tents or eco-cottages.

📍 Padinjarathara (720m) 🍴 Kerala Traditional Lunch ⛺ Dome Tents / Cottages
02:30 PM – 05:00 PM

Banasura Sagar Dam Archipelago Exploration & Boating

▾

Visit the magnificent Banasura Sagar Dam — the largest earth dam in India and second largest in Asia. Enjoy an exhilarating speedboating or kayaking safari through the crystal-clear reservoir waters, navigating between dozens of emerald-green submerged islands with the imposing Banasura mountain towering in the background.

🚤 Speedboat & Kayaking Safari 🌴 Dam Earthen Embankment Walk 📸 Sunset Photography
05:30 PM – 09:30 PM

Spice Trail Walk, Forest Briefing & Wilderness Campfire

▾

Embark on a gentle acclimatization walk through lush cardamom, black pepper, and arabica coffee estates at the base of the peak. At dusk, our certified Kerala Forest Department guide conducts a comprehensive safety and trail briefing, including leech-protection techniques. Gather around a crackling campfire under pristine starlit skies for live acoustic tunes, barbecue snacks, and a restful night's sleep.

🔔 Forest Permit & Safety Protocol 🔥 Campfire BBQ & Acoustic Music 🌟 Milky Way Stargazing
05:30 AM – 07:00 AM

Alpine Dawn, Forest Checkpost & Stream Crossing

▾

Wake up early to the symphony of mountain birds. Enjoy hot Wayanad coffee, organic bananas, and warm porridge. We drive 10 minutes to the Forest Eco-Checkpost (1,040m) where official trekking permits and ecotourism clearances are validated. The trail begins with a gentle hike through rainforest foliage before crossing a pristine boulder-filled mountain stream.

⛰ Start Elevation: 1,040m 🍵 Hot Coffee & Energy Breakfast 🌊 Crystal Forest Stream
07:00 AM – 09:30 AM

Rainforest Ascent to Rolling Shola Grasslands

▾

Hike up through the dense, untouched canopy of the Western Ghats rainforest. Ancient trees adorned with moss and epiphytic orchids filter morning sunbeams. Keep your eyes peeled for the vibrant Malabar Giant Squirrel and exotic bird species. Around 1,450m, the dense jungle gives way dramatically to expansive, undulating Shola grasslands carpeted in wild mountain grass.

🌳 Tropical Evergreen Jungle 🐿 Malabar Giant Squirrel & Hornbills 🌞 Shola Grassland Horizon
09:30 AM – 11:30 AM

Knife-Edge Ridge Traverse to Conical Summit (2,073m)

▾

This is the most thrilling and iconic section of the trek! Follow the narrow knife-edge wind saddle connecting the sub-peaks to the main conical tower of Banasura. Cool mountain winds sweep across the ridge as clouds drift beneath you. The final 150m ascent is a steep scramble over rocky boulders. Stand atop Banasura Peak at 2,073m with a triumphant 360-degree panorama of Banasura Sagar Dam, Chembra, and the Nilgiri ranges.

🚩 Summit Elevation: 2,073m 💨 Windswept Knife-Edge Ridge 🥪 Summit Snacks & Photo Session
12:15 PM – 04:00 PM

Scenic Ridge Descent, Completion Debrief & Farewell

▾

After savoring the summit views and capturing memories, begin the steady, mindful descent back along the grassland spine and rainforest trail. Reach the Forest Checkpost by 2:45 PM. Return to basecamp to freshen up with hot showers, enjoy a celebratory post-trek meal, receive your official Bootpaths Certificate of Completion, and depart with memories of conquering Wayanad's grandest peak.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about permits, fitness, weather, and safety

How difficult is the Banasura Peak trek?

▾

Banasura Peak is categorized as Moderate to Strenuous. While the early plantation trail is gentle, the middle rainforest and the steep conical rocky climb involve an elevation gain of over 1,033 metres over a ~18 km round trip. Anyone with average cardiovascular fitness who can walk 6–7 hours can successfully summit. Prior trekking experience is helpful but not mandatory.

Are Kerala Forest Department permits required, and how are they arranged?

▾

Yes, Banasura Peak falls within the protected Kerala Forest Department North Wayanad division. Trekking without official permission and a certified forest guide is strictly illegal. Bootpaths handles all forest permits, ecotourism clearances, and certified native tribal guides as part of your booking package. You only need to provide a government photo ID (Aadhaar or Passport).

What is the best season to trek Banasura Peak?

▾

The prime window is from September to February. Post-monsoon (September to November) offers vibrant green Shola grasslands, roaring forest streams, and floating cloud strata. Winter (December to February) brings crisp morning visibility, pleasant mountain breezes, and clear summit horizons. The trek is officially closed by the Forest Department during peak monsoon (June–August) for safety.

Are leeches present on the trail?

▾

During September and October (post-monsoon), leeches are active in the lower shaded rainforest and stream sections. Bootpaths provides complimentary high-top leech protection socks and natural herbal repellents (tobacco-salt mixture). Once you cross the tree line into the open Shola grasslands (1,450m+), the trail is completely leech-free.

How do I reach the basecamp in Padinjarathara?

▾

Padinjarathara is located 18 km from Kalpetta (Wayanad's central hub) and 24 km from Mananthavady. Nearest railway station is Kozhikode (Calicut - CLT), approximately 85 km away (around 2.5 hours scenic drive through Thamarassery Ghats). Nearest airport is Kannur International Airport (CNN, 88 km) and Calicut International Airport (CCJ, 95 km). Direct overnight KSRTC sleeper buses operate from Bangalore, Kochi, and Chennai.

Is camping available near the peak?

▾

To protect the fragile Shola grassland ecology and wildlife corridors, pitching tents atop the summit ridge is prohibited by Kerala Forest laws. Bootpaths hosts you at our private, authorized foothill wilderness campsite near Padinjarathara equipped with comfortable alpine dome tents, clean washrooms, campfire zone, and panoramic views of the mountain.
