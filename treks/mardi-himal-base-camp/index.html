About the Trail

Mardi Himal Base Camp
Ridge Expedition

The first day on the trail takes you into the trees. From Pitam Deurali, you walk through rhododendron forest to the teahouses of Forest Camp. Two days later, the trees fall away at Badal Danda and the Annapurna range opens up.

On a clear day, Annapurna South and Hiunchuli rise across the valley, while Machhapuchhre, Nepal's Fishtail peak, fills the skyline ahead. The longest day begins with a headlamp. From High Camp, we follow the ridge past the Upper Viewpoint towards Mardi Himal Base Camp, approximately 4,500 m, if conditions allow, before returning to High Camp for lunch and descending to Low Camp.

Why This Trek

Why Trek Mardi Himal?

Key moments and natural perspectives along the ridge trail beneath Machhapuchhre

01

See Fishtail from an open ridge

Machhapuchhre appears through gaps in the forest and comes fully into view above Low Camp alongside Annapurna South and Hiunchuli.

02

Walk above treeline at Badal Danda

The open ridge opens sweeping views into the Modi Khola valley below and along the shoulder of Machhapuchhre.

03

Catch first light on the upper trail

Set out with a headlamp and watch the outline of the ridge emerge as the sky brightens over the Himalayas.

04

Aim for Base Camp at ~4,500 m

Pause at the Upper Viewpoint (~4,200 m) before continuing higher along the ridge to Mardi Himal Base Camp if conditions allow.

05

See how the landscape changes

Start beneath mossy branches and rhododendrons, cross into open alpine grass and rock above Badal Danda, and return to forest shelter.

06

Four nights in mountain teahouses

End each day with a hot meal and warm company while the ridge cools outside under starlit Himalayan skies.

07

Fields and villages of Kalimati

Walk from forest trails into cultivated hillsides and traditional settlements before driving back to Pokhara.

08

Make time for an inward journey

Quiet stretches on foot give you space to think, while teahouse evenings bring everyone together over stories.

🧭 Official Expedition Trail Route

Mardi Himal Ridge Trail Route

Gorakhpur ➜ Pokhara (~820 m) ➜ Pitam Deurali (~2,100 m) ➜ Forest Camp (~2,550 m) ➜ Low Camp (~3,000 m) ➜ Badal Danda (~3,210 m) ➜ High Camp (~3,600 m) ➜ Upper Viewpoint (~4,200 m) ➜ Mardi Himal Base Camp (~4,500 m) ➜ Low Camp ➜ Kalimati ➜ Pokhara ➜ Gorakhpur

DAY 1

Gorakhpur → Pokhara (~820 m)

Overnight: Pokhara | Meet group in Gorakhpur and travel by road across India–Nepal border. Set aside full day for travel.
DAY 2

Pokhara (~820 m) → Pitam Deurali (~2,100 m) → Forest Camp (~2,550 m)

Walking: 4–5 hrs | Overnight: Forest Camp teahouse | Jeep to Pitam Deurali, climb shaded woodland over stone steps and exposed roots to Forest Camp.
DAY 3

Forest Camp (~2,550 m) → Low Camp (~3,000 m)

Walking: 3–4 hrs | Overnight: Low Camp teahouse | Climb through rhododendron forest via Rest Camp. Settle in at Low Camp with views of Fishtail.
DAY 4

Low Camp (~3,000 m) → Badal Danda (~3,210 m) → High Camp (~3,600 m)

Walking: 4–5 hrs | Overnight: High Camp teahouse | Leave forest behind, follow open ridge to High Camp. Prep gear for pre-dawn summit ridge hike.
DAY 5

High Camp (~3,600 m) → Upper Viewpoint (~4,200 m) → Mardi Himal Base Camp (~4,500 m) → High Camp → Low Camp (~3,000 m)

Walking: 7–10 hrs (full route) | Overnight: Low Camp teahouse | Pre-dawn ridge ascent to Base Camp, return to High Camp for lunch, descend to Low Camp.
DAY 6

Low Camp (~3,000 m) → Kalimati (~1,800 m) → Pokhara (~820 m)

Walking: 5–6 hrs | Overnight: Pokhara | Descend through Rest Camp, branch towards Kalimati, walk through farmland to roadhead, drive to Pokhara.
DAY 7

Pokhara (~820 m) → Gorakhpur

Overnight: Self-arranged in Gorakhpur | Travel by road back to India. Allow for border and highway delays.
DAY 8

Gorakhpur → Hometown

Continue home by train, flight, or other onward transport with unforgettable memories of the Mardi ridge.

Set out before dawn with headlamps. Continue beyond the Upper Viewpoint to Base Camp (~4,500 m) if trail conditions and pace allow, standing directly beneath Machhapuchhre. Return to High Camp for lunch, then descend via Badal Danda to Low Camp.
