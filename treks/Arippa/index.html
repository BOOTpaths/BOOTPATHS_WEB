About the Trek

Kerala's Living Prehistoric Swamp
& Birdwatcher's Eden

Nestled along the verdant foothills of the Agasthyamalai Biosphere Reserve in Kollam-Trivandrum border, Arippa is an ecological wonderland sheltering one of the planet's rarest freshwater swamp ecosystems.

The crown jewel of Arippa is its ancient Myristica Swamp — a living relic dating back millions of years to the prehistoric era. As you step onto the elevated forest walkways, you are surrounded by giant wild nutmeg trees with bizarre aerial stilt roots and knee-like breathing roots (pneumatophores) standing submerged in slow-flowing, crystal freshwater streams.

Arippa is equally celebrated worldwide as a paradise for ornithologists and birders, harboring over 270 bird species. Led by indigenous Kani tribal naturalists and certified Bootpaths guides, you will traverse dense tropical evergreen and moist deciduous teak-rosewood canopies, spotting rare Western Ghats endemics like the Malabar Grey Hornbill, Ceylon Frogmouth, Malabar Trogon, and Fairy Bluebird.

Detailed Schedule & Activity Breakdown

Comprehensive step-by-step breakdown of the Arippa rainforest expedition

▾
Morning

Phase 1: Arrival, Eco-Clearance & Myristica Swamp Walk

▸
Trekkers arrive at the Arippa Forest Eco-Tourism Center on the Trivandrum-Shenkottai State Highway (SH 2). After identity verification and Kerala Forest Department clearance, our guides equip everyone with protective leech gaiters and provide an insightful orientation on the sanctuary's sensitive ecology. The first leg introduces you to the prehistoric Myristica swamp, where waterlogged soils sustain ancient tree species that have survived since the Jurassic epoch.
Mid-Day

Phase 2: Core Birding Circuit & Riverbank Feast

▸
We delve deeper into the Sankhili forest zone where the canopy rises over 30 meters high. Guided by sharp-eyed Kani tribal trackers, we look out for the rare Ceylon Frogmouth camouflaged on dry branches, along with the Malabar Grey Hornbill, Emerald Dove, and Oriental Dwarf Kingfisher. At mid-day, we halt beside a tranquil mountain stream to savor a traditional Kerala vegetarian feast packed in biodegradable plantain leaves.
Afternoon

Phase 3: Upper Ridge Trail, Botanical Lore & Farewell

▸
Post lunch, the trail gradually climbs through moist deciduous patches and bamboo corridors to a panoramic forest clearing at 480 meters elevation. The guide explains local ethnobotany, including the medicinal wonders of wild flora like Arogyapacha (Trichopus zeylanicus). We descend through a shaded loop track back to the Arippa eco-center for hot herbal refreshments and group certificates.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trekking and birdwatching in Arippa Forest Reserve

Why is Arippa internationally famous for Myristica Swamps and birdwatching?

+

Arippa contains one of the few surviving Myristica Swamps in the world — an ancient, primitive freshwater wetland ecosystem dating back millions of years, populated by endangered wild nutmeg trees with distinctive aerial stilt roots. Furthermore, its unique micro-climate makes it home to over 270 bird species, including rare Western Ghats endemics like the Ceylon Frogmouth, Malabar Grey Hornbill, and Malabar Trogon.

Is prior booking required for the Arippa Eco-Trek?

+

Yes. As Arippa is an eco-sensitive forest zone governed by the Kerala Forest and Wildlife Department, daily entries are regulated to protect wildlife habitats. Bootpaths coordinates all official forest permits, tribal guide allocations, and logistic arrangements in advance.

What is the fitness level required for this trek? Can beginners and families join?

+

The Arippa Eco-Trek is rated Easy to Moderate. The circuit spans approximately 8 km across shaded, gently undulating forest pathways, elevated boardwalks, and stream crossings. It is perfect for beginners, photographers, birdwatching enthusiasts, families, and children above 7 years who enjoy nature walks.

What is the best season and time of day for the trek?

+

The prime season is from October to May, when the monsoon subsides, forest streams are pristine, and winter migratory birds arrive in abundance. Early morning (07:00 AM) is the optimal departure time to experience peak avian activity and serene swamp mist.

How do I reach the Arippa Basecamp?

+

The Arippa Forest Eco-Tourism Center is conveniently located on the Thiruvananthapuram – Shenkottai State Highway (SH 2), near Kulathupuzha and Madathara. It is approximately 52 km from Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum) city and 58 km from Kollam. Well-paved highway connectivity makes it easily accessible by private car or KSRTC buses. Bootpaths also arranges shared or dedicated vehicle pickups upon request.

What environmental and plastic policies are enforced?

+

The reserve adheres to strict Zero-Plastic and eco-conservation rules. Single-use plastic bottles, packets, smoking, and loud speakers are prohibited. Fresh filtered water refills are provided at base camp, and all trekkers must carry reusable water bottles.
