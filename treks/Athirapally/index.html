About the Trek

Kerala's Pristine
High-Canopy & Cliff Ridge Expedition

The Athirappilly–Vazhachal forest corridor is renowned across the globe as one of the Western Ghats' most vibrant ecological sanctuaries, where mist-veiled Sholayar ranges shelter untamed rainforests and the emerald Chalakudy River.

Nadukani — translating in Malayalam to "to view the entire country" — is a historic rocky ridge towering over the surrounding jungle. Reaching this vantage point demands navigating an exhilarating 8.5 km trail through wet evergreen jungle, colossal ancient buttress trees, wild bamboo thickets, and bubbling forest streams.

Accompanied by Kerala Forest Department certified indigenous Kadar tribal naturalists and expert Bootpaths trek leads, you will learn the ancient secrets of the forest, track footprints of Asian elephants and sambar deer, and witness the majesty of India's premier hornbill habitat — where all four South Indian hornbill species soar freely above the canopies.

Four Hornbill Species Haven

Briefing, Forest Permits & Lowland Rain Forest Canopy

Assemble bright and early at the Vazhachal Eco-Tourism desk. Bootpaths handles all official Forest Department entry permissions, VSS clearance, and assigns your certified Kadar tribal guides.

Following an essential safety orientation and leech gaiter demonstration, step into the lush moist deciduous and semi-evergreen forest zone. The trail winds parallel to the upper reaches of the Chalakudy river catchment under a cool, shaded leafy roof.

Listen for the loud resonant wing-whooshes of the Great Indian Hornbill and observe the vibrant plumage of the Malabar Trogon and Emerald Dove darting through the undergrowth.

Rock Hopping, Cool Waters & Ascent to the Viewpoint

Phase 2 presents the heart of the adventure. The trail crosses shimmering mountain tributaries flowing through natural granite sluices, where trekkers can pause to refresh with pure forest water.

Beyond the streams, enter a vast stretch of wild bamboo brakes. The gradient steepens as you navigate natural root steps and rocky switchbacks carved into the hillside by indigenous hunter-gatherers over generations.

As the canopy thins out, refreshing mountain breezes signal your arrival at the high ridge. Reach the summit plateau of Nadukani at 850m and unpack traditional Kerala trail meals wrapped in banana leaves.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trekking in Athirapally and ascending the Nadukani Ridge

Is prior booking required for the Athirapally Nadukani Trek?

+

Yes. Because the Vazhachal and Sholayar forest zones are strictly protected reserves managed by the Kerala Forest and Wildlife Department, daily trekker batches are strictly capped to conserve the fragile hornbill ecosystem. Bootpaths secures all official government permits, VSS approvals, and indigenous Kadar guides well ahead of your trek date.

What is the fitness level required for this trek?

+

The Athirapally Nadukani Trek is graded as Moderate. The total trail distance is approximately 8.5 km round-trip, with an elevation gain of around 530 meters up to the 850m crest. It is suitable for active beginners, fitness enthusiasts, nature photographers, and seasoned hikers. Regular brisk walkers or anyone with average cardiovascular stamina can comfortably finish the trek.

How do I reach the trek basecamp at Vazhachal?

+

The starting point is at the Vazhachal Forest Range Office, located just 5 km past the renowned Athirappilly Waterfalls. The nearest major railway station is Chalakudy (CKI - 36 km) or Thrissur (TCR - 65 km). The nearest airport is Cochin International Airport (COK - 55 km). Bootpaths can also coordinate private or shared group transfers directly from Kochi or Thrissur upon request.

Are leeches common along the trail, and what protection is provided?

+

During the post-monsoon months (September to November), damp rainforest humus can harbor harmless jungle leeches. Bootpaths provides every trekker with certified woven leech gaiters (socks) and natural herbal repellent sprays (tobacco and salt mixtures) that prevent leeches from attaching to skin.

Can children and families participate in the Nadukani trek?

+

Children aged 10 years and above who are accustomed to outdoor walking can participate with adult supervision. It offers an exceptional educational experience in biodiversity, birdwatching, and indigenous forest knowledge.

What ecological and zero-plastic policies must be followed?

+

The Kerala Forest Department enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against plastic littering, smoking, alcohol, and music speakers in the Vazhachal division. Single-use plastic water bottles and snack packets are strictly prohibited. Trekkers must carry reusable metal flasks or sturdy bottles; fresh filtered drinking water refills are provided at basecamp.
