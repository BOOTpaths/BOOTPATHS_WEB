About the Trek

Kerala's Crown Jewel of
Untouched Eco-Tourism

Gavi is an enchanting high-altitude ecological sanctuary tucked deep inside the pristine Ranni Reserve Forest and Periyar Tiger Reserve buffer zone in Pathanamthitta district.

Acclaimed internationally for its model conservation framework run by the Kerala Forest Development Corporation (KFDC), the Gavi Eco-Trek offers a deeply restorative communion with pure nature: trekking through high-altitude shola grasslands and aromatic cardamom plantations, followed by silent row-boating on the placid, mirror-clear Gavi lake.

Accompanied by certified local tribal trackers and Bootpaths wilderness expedition leads, you will ascend scenic cliff lookouts including the famed Sabarimala viewpoint, discover secluded waterfalls, and spot wildlife — such as wild Asian elephant herds, the endangered Lion-tailed Macaque, Nilgiri Tahr, and vibrant Malabar Giant Squirrels.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about trekking and safari in Gavi Wildlife Sanctuary

Is prior official permit reservation mandatory for Gavi?

+

Yes. Gavi is a highly sensitive ecological zone within the Periyar Tiger Reserve buffer. The Kerala Forest Development Corporation (KFDC) and Kerala Forest Department restrict daily visitor entry through strict quotas. Bootpaths takes care of all advance entry permits, tribal guide allocations, safari clearances, and boating slots on your behalf.

What is the fitness level required for the Gavi trek?

+

The trek is graded as Easy to Moderate. The trails traverse gentle plantation paths, shaded shola canopies, and stream banks with moderate gradients. It is ideal for families, photographers, nature enthusiasts, and first-time hikers. Children aged 6 and above can comfortably participate.

Is boating on Gavi Lake safe, and are lifejackets provided?

+

Safety is strictly enforced. All boating and canoeing activities are operated under the guidance of certified local oarsmen. High-grade ISI-certified personal flotation devices (lifejackets) are mandatory for every trekker before boarding.

How do I reach the Gavi starting basecamp?

+

Gavi is accessed via the Vallakkadavu checkpost near Vandiperiyar / Kumily (approx. 28 km) or via Angamoozhy in Pathanamthitta district. The nearest railway stations are Kottayam (115 km) and Chengannur (120 km). The nearest airports are Cochin International Airport (COK - 165 km) and Madurai (IXM - 150 km). Bootpaths can arrange shared or private safari vehicle transfers.

What wildlife might we encounter in Gavi?

+

Gavi is celebrated for sightings of wild Asian elephant herds, the endangered Lion-tailed Macaque, Nilgiri Tahr, Indian Gaur (bison), Sambar deer, Malabar Giant Squirrels, and over 260 bird species including the Great Indian Hornbill and Malabar Whistling Thrush.

What eco-regulations and plastic rules are in place?

+

Gavi is a strictly protected plastic-free sanctuary. Single-use plastic water bottles, disposable polythene bags, alcohol, and music speakers are strictly prohibited. Trekkers must carry reusable flasks, which can be refilled with filtered drinking water at the basecamp.
