About the Trail

Ascent of the Second Highest Peak:
Rhodo Valley to the Summit

The day begins with a drive from Aluva to Munnar, followed by a jeep ride along tea-estate roads to the Kerala Forest Development Corporation (KFDC) Base Camp and onward to Rhodo Valley. From there, the trail passes rhododendron trees and pockets of shola forest before opening onto rolling high-altitude grasslands.

The terrain alternates between climbs and dips, with a steeper pull up to the summit ridge. Meesapulimala is the second-highest peak in the Western Ghats, after Anamudi. On a clear morning, the summit opens up views towards Munnar, Suryanelli, and the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border hills. The group descends along the same trail to Rhodo Valley, where jeeps pick us up for the ride back to Base Camp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Meesapulimala Day Trek

Is the jeep ride included in ₹4,399?

▼

Yes, includes jeep transfers between Munnar and Rhodo Valley via Base Camp and road transfer between Aluva and Munnar.

Is this suitable for a first-time trekker?

▼

Active beginners comfortable walking 8–10 km on hilly ground can consider it; expect repeated climbs, a steep final approach, and midnight-start fatigue.

Do I need to book a KFDC slot separately?

▼

Package covers KFDC trek entry and local guide; carry a government-issued photo ID.

Will I see Neelakurinji or a sea of clouds?

▼

Both depend on seasonal cycles and morning weather; photograph any flowers from the trail without disturbing plants.

What should I carry for the early start?

▼

Grippy trekking shoes, warm layer for pre-dawn jeep ride/ridge, light rain jacket, headlamp, ~2L water in a reusable bottle, and personal medication.

Does midnight pickup mean I should come the previous evening?

▼

Yes, e.g., for a Sunday trek, reach Aluva Railway Station by 11:45 PM on Saturday.

What happens if the summit route is restricted?

▼

Trek leader follows KFDC access decisions and local guide instructions; weather/visibility may require an earlier turnaround.
