About the Trek

Kerala's Pristine
High-Altitude Wilderness

Nestled within the UNESCO-recognized Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve, Kathirmudi is a soaring crown peak that separates the misty tea plantations of Bonacaud from the dense, virgin rainforests of the Peppara and Agasthyarkoodam wilderness.

The Kathirmudi Trek offers a rare, multi-layered trekking experience. You begin among the nostalgic ruins of colonial British tea bungalows and terraced tea gardens before delving into shadowy primary shola forests flanked by ancient mossy trees, wild orchids, and murmuring freshwater cascades.

As the forest gives way to open alpine grasslands, you negotiate a rewarding ridge scramble to reach the windswept summit of Kathirmudi (1,120m). Here, you are rewarded with 360-degree vistas stretching across the Peppara reservoir basin, Ponmudi hill station, and the sacred pyramid peak of Agasthyarkoodam.

Detailed Phase-by-Phase Route

In-depth breakdown of trail stages, terrain conditions & guide notes

Comprehensive
Phase 01

Bonacaud Base Camp to Lower Shola Gateway

Trailhead Clearance • 480m to 750m • ~3.5 km

The expedition kicks off at the Bonacaud Forest Station, nestled in the scenic foothills near Vithura. Trekkers are welcomed with refreshing local tea and complete identity verification for Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve entry permits. The initial leg winds through gentle terraced slopes of the historic British tea estate, providing panoramic glimpses of the mist-cloaked Kathirmudi massif looming above.

Forest Checkpost Clearance: Biometric verification, bag check for single-use plastic disposal, and safety protocol alignment.
Colonial Bungalow Pass: Hike past the stone relics of 19th-century planter architecture and towering silver oak canopy avenues.
Transition to Wild Shola: The manicured tea rows yield to thick evergreen tree lines, marking your entry into the core wildlife corridor.
💡
Naturalist Pro-Tip: Apply leech gaiters or eucalyptus/snuff powder before stepping past the tea boundary into the moist leaf-litter trails.
Phase 02

Deep Rainforest, Stream Cascades & Ridge Ascent

Heart of Shola • 750m to 1,020m • ~3 km

Leaving the open slopes behind, the trail plunges under a cathedral of ancient tropical trees. Giant lianas twist across massive hardwood trunks draped in rare hanging moss. You will cross two crystal-clear mountain streams fed by high-altitude springs, offering refreshing resting spots before the trail steepens into a cardiovascular ascent through natural rocky root-steps.

Kallar Catchment Stream Crossing: Step across smooth granite boulders with cool, drinkable natural mineral water flowing straight from the hills.
Giant Bamboo Corridor: Hike beneath soaring clusters of wild bamboo whose creaking in the wind creates an enchanting wilderness orchestra.
Wildlife Canopy Lookout: Frequent sightings of the maroon-and-black Malabar Giant Squirrel and Nilgiri Langurs high in the canopy branches.
⚠️
Terrain Notice: The climb involves natural root scrambles and a 30-degree slope. Trekking poles provide tremendous stability here.
Phase 03

Kathirmudi Summit Ridge & Panoramic Crest

Summit Pinnacle • 1,020m to 1,120m • ~3.5 km Loop

The dense shola jungle abruptly gives way to expansive, wind-sculpted grasslands. A final 20-minute ridge scramble over solid granite brings you to the summit cairn of Kathirmudi. At 1,120 meters, the view opens up into a dramatic 360-degree panorama: looking west at the shimmering blue expanse of the Peppara reservoir, north towards Ponmudi, and south-east towards the revered peak of Agasthyarkoodam.

High Alpine Grassland Crest: Walk along the narrow grassy ridge with cool mountain gusts blowing up from the valley below.
Summit Pinnacle Vantage: 360-degree sweeping vista of the Western Ghats mountain spine, cloud inversions, and reservoir basin.
Summit Lunch & Photography: Unpack your eco-friendly banana-leaf packed Kerala meal while savoring the untouched silence of the peaks.
🌿
Eco Code: Strictly zero-trace zone. All wrappers, peelings, and belongings must be carried back to base camp for recycling.

Trail Regulations & Safety Guide

Permit guidelines, weather advisories & responsible eco-trek protocols

Must Read
📜

Forest Permits & Entry Rules

Kathirmudi is a protected zone within the Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve. All entries require prior permission from the Kerala Forest Department & Vana Samrakshana Samithi (VSS). Bootpaths handles all advance booking permits and entry passes on your behalf.

👞

Footwear & Leeches

Sturdy trekking shoes with high-traction lug soles are strongly advised due to wet rocks and grass roots. The shola forest section has forest leeches during post-monsoon months; leech socks, salt, and tobacco dust will be provided by your guide.

⚖️

Fitness Requirement

Rated as Moderate. Requires reasonable stamina for 5–6 hours of walking with intermittent steep ascents. Suitable for regular hikers, active families with teenagers, and beginner trekkers with good baseline fitness.

♻️

Strict Plastic-Free Policy

The sanctuary enforces a 100% ban on single-use plastic bottles, snack wrappers, and polythene. Trekkers must carry reusable metallic or BPA-free water bottles. Forest guards will inspect baggage at the Bonacaud entry gate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking the Kathirmudi Trek

The Kathirmudi Trek is rated as Moderate. The total distance is approximately 10 km round trip, starting from 480m elevation at Bonacaud and ascending to 1,120m at the summit. While the tea estate section is gentle, the shola forest and grassland ridge involve steady inclines and uneven rocky terrain. Anyone with average cardiovascular fitness and active lifestyle can complete it comfortably.

The premier trekking window is from October to May. Post-monsoon (October–January) offers vivid green hills, active streams, and dramatic cloudbeds. The drier months (February–May) provide crystal clear horizon views extending to the Arabian Sea and distant Western Ghats peaks. Trekking is subject to forest department weather restrictions during heavy monsoon peaks.

Bonacaud is located approximately 55 km from Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum) city. You can reach Bonacaud via Nedumangad – Vithura. Private cabs, self-drive vehicles, and early morning KSRTC buses from Trivandrum / Nedumangad operate to Vithura / Bonacaud. Bootpaths can also arrange shared group pickups from Trivandrum upon request.

Yes. Kathirmudi falls under the eco-fragile Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve and Peppara Wildlife division. Entry is strictly regulated with limited daily passes issued by the Kerala Forest Department. When you book with Bootpaths, all mandatory department permits, guide allocations, and safety clearances are secured in advance.

Children aged 10 and above with active outdoor experience are welcome. Seniors with good health, no severe knee/cardiac issues, and comfortable with moderate ascents can also enjoy the trek. Trekking poles are highly recommended for additional knee support on descents.
