About The Expedition

The Crown Jewel of the
Eastern Himalayas

Nestled within the UNESCO World Heritage realm of Kanchenjunga National Park in West Sikkim, the Goechala Trek is widely revered as India's most breathtaking high-altitude mountain expedition.

Starting from the historical coronation capital of Yuksom, the trail weaves upward across roaring glacial torrents of the Prek Chu river, ascending through primeval moss-draped oak forests and world-famous crimson rhododendron groves at Tshoka and Phedang.

As you cross into alpine tundra above 13,000 feet, you are greeted by the awe-inspiring 360-degree panorama from Dzongri Top, the mystical stillness of Samiti Lake, and the ultimate sunrise summit at Goecha La Viewpoint 1 (15,100 ft) — where the mammoth south-eastern wall of Mount Kanchenjunga (8,586m) looms so close it feels within touching distance.

360-Degree Panoramic Himalayan Amphitheater • Critical Acclimatization

Wake up at 4:30 AM with headlamps for the steep 45-minute climb to Dzongri Top (13,680 ft). As the first rays of dawn strike the snow-clad peaks, watch the world turn from deep purple to blazing gold across a breathtaking 360-degree panorama.

Behold Mount Kanchenjunga (8,586m), Mount Pandim (6,691m), Kabru North (7,338m), Kabru South (7,317m), Koktang, Rathong, Frey Peak, and Simvo. Spend the afternoon resting, drinking warm garlic soup, and acclimatizing for the higher camps ahead.

🍁 Autumn Season (Late Sept – November)

Crystal clear sapphire skies with razor-sharp visibility of Kanchenjunga and surrounding peaks. Very low precipitation. Day temperatures range from 10°C to 15°C; Night temperatures drop from 2°C down to -8°C at higher camps.

🌸 Spring Season (March – May)

World-renowned blooming rhododendron forests in full crimson, pink, and white glory. Warmer daylight temperatures (12°C to 18°C). Clear early mornings with crisp mountain vistas and vibrant birdlife.

How to Reach Yuksom & Transit Logistics

Flight, train, and road connectivity guidelines from Bagdogra (IXB) and NJP Station

✈️ By Air (Bagdogra Airport - IXB)

Bagdogra Airport (IXB) in Siliguri is well-connected with direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bangalore, and Hyderabad. Yuksom is 150 km (~6.5 hrs drive) from Bagdogra via Melli and Jorethang.

🚆 By Train (New Jalpaiguri - NJP)

New Jalpaiguri Railway Station (NJP) is the primary railhead connecting all major Indian cities. Bootpaths coordinates shared SUV pickups from NJP station at 09:30 AM on Day 1.

Bootpaths Transit Support: We provide dedicated shared or private mountain SUV pickup and return transfers directly between NJP / Bagdogra and Yuksom. Select this add-on during booking or request via WhatsApp.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for and trekking the Goechala Pass in West Sikkim

How difficult is the Goechala Pass Trek and who can join?

+

The Goechala Trek is classified as Challenging / Difficult due to its high altitude (15,100 ft), continuous multi-day duration (9 days), and daily distances of 8 to 18 km across rugged Himalayan terrain. While prior trekking experience in the Himalayas is recommended, fit beginners who maintain consistent cardiovascular fitness (running 5 km in under 30 minutes, stamina conditioning, and leg workouts) for 6 to 8 weeks prior can successfully complete the trek under Bootpaths guidance.

Why does the trek turn around at Goecha La Viewpoint 1 (15,100 ft)?

+

Historically, trekkers continued past Viewpoint 1 towards Viewpoint 2 and the actual Goecha La Pass. However, the Sikkim Forest Department and Kanchenjunga National Park authorities now strictly designate Viewpoint 1 (15,100 ft / 4,600m) as the official and safest viewing summit. Viewpoint 1 offers by far the most majestic, unobstructed 180-degree amphitheater of Mt. Kanchenjunga's southeastern face, without the hazardous loose scree and rockfall dangers of the higher unmaintained ridge.

How does Bootpaths manage altitude safety and AMS (Acute Mountain Sickness)?

+

Safety is Bootpaths' paramount priority. Our itinerary includes dedicated acclimatization stops at Tshoka (9,710 ft) and Dzongri (13,025 ft). Our certified Mountaineering Leads conduct mandatory twice-daily pulse oximeter readings (SpO2 & pulse rate) and health assessments. Our expedition teams carry medical-grade oxygen cylinders, portable hyperbaric kits, and comprehensive high-altitude emergency first aid at all times.

What permits and identity documents are required?

+

For Indian Nationals: A valid Government photo ID (Passport, Aadhaar Card, or Voter ID), 4 passport-size photographs, and a signed Medical Fitness Certificate. Bootpaths processes all Kanchenjunga National Park and Sikkim Forest Department permits in Yuksom.

For Foreign Nationals: An Indian Visa, Inner Line Permit (ILP) for Sikkim, Restricted Area Permit (RAP), and a minimum group size of 2 foreign trekkers as mandated by Sikkim state regulations.

What are the camping and toilet arrangements on the trail?

+

Accommodation is in premium 4-season high-altitude dome tents (twin sharing) with warm sub-zero sleeping bags and thermal insulation mattresses. At every campsite, separate dry toilet tents with organic sawdust pit systems are set up adhering strictly to Leave No Trace environmental standards. At Yuksom basecamp, you stay in comfortable traditional guesthouses with hot showers.

Is mobile network connectivity available during the trek?

+

Mobile network (primarily Airtel, Jio, and BSNL) is available in Yuksom. Once you cross the forest checkpoint into Kanchenjunga National Park on Day 2, there is zero cellular connectivity for the rest of the trek until returning to Yuksom on Day 9. Our trek leaders carry satellite communication and forest wireless radio links for emergency coordination.
