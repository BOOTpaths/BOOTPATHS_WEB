About the Trek

The King of the Western Ghats:
Pushpagiri Sanctuary Expedition

Known as the King of the Western Ghats, Kumaraparvatha rises to 1,712 metres in Karnataka's Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary. On this BOOTpaths trek, we approach the summit from Beedalli and descend along the same trail. The climb takes us through dense forest and up rocky stretches. When the clouds lift, the summit opens to sweeping views of the surrounding hills.

We spend the first afternoon settling into our homestay over a home-cooked lunch, visiting a private waterfall and getting to know the group around a campfire. The next morning, we take on the full 14 km trek together. Expect a sustained climb, a descent that asks for steady footing, and time to pause, catch your breath and take in the forest along the way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Kumaraparvatha trek via Beedalli

How difficult is the Beedalli route?

▼

It's a challenging 14 km out-and-back trek completed in a single day. Expect a sustained uphill climb and rocky stretches where footing matters, especially on the descent.

Can I join if I'm new to trekking?

▼

If you already walk or exercise regularly and are comfortable spending several hours on your feet, you can consider it. A few practice hikes with uphill and downhill sections will help you gauge your fitness before signing up.

Where exactly is the pickup point?

▼

Meet at Mangaluru Central Railway Station (MAQ) for the 9:00 AM pickup on Day 1.

Can I book a return train for Day 2?

▼

The vehicle is scheduled to leave the homestay at 3:30 PM. The drive to Mangaluru takes several hours, and traffic or a later finish on the trail can affect arrival. Allow a generous buffer for an onward train or plan to leave the following morning.

What documents should I bring?

▼

Carry an original government-issued photo ID for verification at the trailhead. Use the same name and ID details when sharing your details during registration.

Where will we stay overnight?

▼

The itinerary includes an overnight stop at a local homestay near Beedalli. Check with BOOTpaths for room-sharing arrangements and stay coverage details.

What meals are provided?

▼

Day 1 lunch and dinner, Day 2 breakfast, and a packed lunch for summit day are included. Share any dietary requirements with BOOTpaths in advance so the team can check what the homestay can accommodate.

What should I carry for summit day?

▼

Bring trekking shoes with good grip, a small daypack, reusable water bottles, rainwear, a cap, a spare pair of socks, personal medication and trail snacks. A trekking pole can help on the descent. Carry your wrappers back and follow the forest guides' instructions throughout the trek.

Can everyone get into the waterfall?

▼

The dip is optional and depends on rainfall, water levels and local conditions. You can enjoy the visit from the banks if you prefer to stay dry.

What happens if the weather changes?

▼

The trek leader and local forest guides may adjust the route timings or turn the group around based on weather, trail conditions and Forest Department instructions.
