About the Expedition

South India's Sacred
Wilderness Sanctuary

The Agastyarkoodam expedition by Bootpaths is an immersive trek into the core of the Western Ghats. Spanning over 50 kilometers across three days, this high-endurance trail navigates dense evergreen forests, pristine mountain streams, and steep rock faces to reach Kerala’s second-highest peak at 1,868 metres.

UNESCO Biosphere Hotspot: The trail traverses untouched tropical evergreen rainforests, bamboo groves, and cloud-capped grasslands within the UNESCO-recognized Agasthyamalai Biosphere Reserve. Home to over 2,000 species of medicinal plants and rare orchids, the region acts as a living botanical repository. Trekkers routinely spot endemic wildlife, including the Nilgiri Tahr along higher crags, the Great Hornbill, the Malabar Trogon, and wild elephant herds roaming lower valleys.

The Technical Summit Push: The final ascent to Agastyarkoodam Peak tests physical conditioning and upper-body agility. The final stretch transitions into sheer, near-vertical rock faces where the Forest Department has installed heavy-duty fixed ropes. Negotiating these granite walls requires focus, balance, and controlled movement.

Spiritual & Panoramic Summit: Named after Sage Agastya from ancient Indian traditions, the summit holds a small stone shrine surrounded by windswept ridges. On clear days, the peak delivers 360-degree views across the Neyyar and Peppara dam reservoirs, rolling forest canopy, and the distant glint of the Arabian Sea.

Detailed Day-by-Day Route Guide

Complete daily narrative, elevation gains, checkpoint rules, and gear notes

Day 1: Bonacaud Picket Station to Athirumala Base Camp

📏 Distance: ~16 km / 10 miles ⏱️ Time: 5 to 7 hours 🌲 Terrain: Mud trails, dense canopy, streams

Trekkers report early to the Bonacaud Forest Checkpoint for mandatory permit verification and strict plastic luggage audits. The journey begins along a wide estate path before diving into dense semi-evergreen and moist deciduous rainforests.

You will cross three pristine mountain streams — Karamanayar, Vazhapanthiyar, and Attayar. Attayar serves as the primary resting and lunch point where you can refresh by the cool, crystal-clear mountain cascades.

Past Attayar, the trail narrows and turns upward through bamboo brakes and dense rainforest canopy, eventually opening up to the elephant-trench protected clearing of Athirumala Base Camp at ~1,000 metres.

🏕️ Overnight: Basic Forest Department dormitories at Athirumala Base Camp (shared floor bedding).

Day 2: Athirumala to Agastyarkoodam Peak and Return

📏 Distance: 10 to 12 km round-trip ⏱️ Time: 6 to 8 hours 🪢 Terrain: Steep ridges, bare granite, fixed ropes

Starting early in the dawn mist, the trail climbs steeply through orchid-rich dwarf shola forests toward Pongalapara. Beyond Pongalapara, the forest thins out into windswept bare rock ridges with dramatic vertical drop-offs.

The final push requires scaling near-vertical granite cliffs using heavy-duty fixed safety ropes installed by forest authorities. Negotiating these walls demands steady footing, upper-body strength, and focus.

At the 1,868-meter summit, trekkers are rewarded with sweeping 360-degree views of the Western Ghats, the Neyyar & Peppara reservoirs, the glint of the Arabian Sea, and the sacred stone shrine of Sage Agastya. After taking in the summit majesty, the team carefully descends using the ropes, returning to Athirumala Base Camp before sunset.

🏕️ Overnight: Athirumala Base Camp (Dinner at forest canteen).

Day 3: Athirumala Base Camp to Bonacaud (Trivandrum Drop)

📏 Distance: ~16 km / 10 miles ⏱️ Time: 4 to 5 hours 🌳 Terrain: Continuous forest descent

Following early morning tea at the base camp canteen, the return journey retraces the trail down through the lush canopy. The quiet morning light offers prime birdwatching opportunities for species like the Great Hornbill and Malabar Trogon.

Arriving at the Bonacaud Picket Station by midday, trekkers undergo a mandatory plastic clearance audit — ensuring all non-biodegradable items declared during entry are accounted for and brought back out.

After completing sign-out formalities with forest officials, the Bootpaths team facilitates private transfers or bus connections back to Trivandrum city / airport.

🏁 Expedition Concludes: Drop-off at Thiruvananthapuram (TVC / TRV).
Logistics & Travel

How to Reach Base Camp

The expedition starts from Bonacaud Forest Picket Station, situated 50–60 km northeast of Thiruvananthapuram (Trivandrum), Kerala.

✈️

By Air

Fly into Trivandrum International Airport (TRV), located ~65 km from the Bonacaud trailhead. Bootpaths coordinates convenient private airport pickups and hotel transfers.

🚂

By Rail

The nearest major railway terminal is Thiruvananthapuram Central (TVC), ~60 km away, connected to all major Indian cities with superfast express trains.

🚌

By Road & Bus

Drive from Trivandrum via Nedumangad & Vithura toward Bonacaud. The final stretch winds through the picturesque old Bonacaud Tea Estate. Early morning KSRTC buses (5:00 AM) depart from Thampanoor Bus Stand directly to Bonacaud.

Packing Essentials

Things to Carry

Trekkers carry their own daypack for all 3 days (porter services are strictly restricted by forest conservation rules). Pack light and smart!

Category Recommended Gear Purpose & Conservation Notes
👕 Clothing 1–2 Quick-Dry T-shirts & Trek Pants Lightweight, breathable, moisture-wicking synthetic fabric suitable for humid tropical forest trekking.
🧥 Warm Layer 1 Fleece / Lightweight Windcheater Evenings and early mornings at Athirumala Base Camp (1,000m) can get surprisingly cool and windy.
🌧️ Rain Protection Poncho / Waterproof Rain Jacket Rainforest weather is dynamic; sudden tropical showers can occur year-round.
🧖 Footwear High-Traction Hiking Shoes + Extra Socks Deep-lugged soles are crucial for muddy roots, slippery stream crossings, and friction on granite slopes.
💧 Hydration 2 × 1-Litre Reusable Bottles / Hydration Bladder Single-use plastic bottles are strictly prohibited by Kerala Forest Dept. Checkpoint staff will confiscate them.
🩺 Personal Care Leech Protection & Basic First Aid Leech socks or tobacco/salt pouches, blister bandages, personal prescription medications, and antiseptic cream.
🔆 Lighting & Power Headlamp & 10,000+ mAh Power Bank Base camp solar power is minimal and charging slots are scarce; headlamp essential for pre-dawn summit push.
🆔 Identification Original Government Photo ID & Permit Printout Mandatory for identity verification at the Bonacaud entry picket station against the official forest e-pass list.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about permits, fitness, forest rules, and camp stay

Entry is strictly regulated by the Kerala Forest Department to safeguard the biosphere reserve. Permits are released via the official Forest Department portal prior to the trekking season (typically open from January through March). Due to intense demand and daily quota caps (usually capped at ~100 trekkers/day), slots sell out within minutes. Bootpaths handles the complete permit registration process for our booked expedition clients.

Due to the requirement of covering 50+ kilometers over three days — including steep vertical climbs using fixed ropes on Day 2 — this trek is recommended for active individuals, regular gym-goers, or beginners with strong cardiovascular endurance. We recommend 3–4 weeks of cardio training (running, stair climbing) prior to the trek.

Accommodation consists of simple forest dormitories with concrete floors and basic sleeping mats. Female trekkers are provided separate, dedicated dormitory quarters. Basic hot vegetarian meals (rice, traditional Kerala sambar/curries, kanji porridge) and tea are freshly prepared and served at the base camp canteen.

Agastyarkoodam is a zero-plastic, strictly protected ecological zone. All plastic items (wrappers, snack packets) must be declared at the Bonacaud checkpoint and accounted for upon return. Disposable plastic water bottles, alcohol, cigarettes/beedis, matchboxes, and bluetooth speakers are strictly prohibited on the trail and will lead to permit cancellation or legal fines.

During damp conditions and along stream crossings (like Attayar), forest leeches are common. We recommend wearing high-ankle leech socks over your hiking socks, tucking pants in, and carrying small pouches of salt or tobacco powder for quick detachment without pulling.

Network coverage drops off shortly past the Bonacaud tea estate. There is virtually no mobile signal or data connectivity along the trail or at Athirumala Base Camp (intermittent BSNL signal is occasionally found on higher ridges). Enjoy this pristine digital detox in the lap of wilderness!
