About the Trek

Where Sacred Legends Meet
Untamed Shola Wilds

Perched majestically along the misty border of Kodagu (Coorg) in Karnataka and Wayanad in Kerala, Brahmagiri is one of South India's most ecologically rich and culturally revered mountain sanctuaries.

The trek begins at the sacred foothill town of Iruppu, where the roaring Lakshmana Tirtha river plunges 170 feet through dense green cliffs. Legend speaks of Lord Rama and Lakshmana visiting these slopes, where an arrow pierced the mountain to unveil eternal healing waters.

As you hike beyond the spray of Iruppu Falls, the forest envelops you in towering Western Ghats Shola trees draped in wild moss and orchids. You then emerge into the open, wind-swept rolling grasslands of the high ridge, hiking along the spine of the hills up to the Brahmagiri Summit at 1,608m, where clouds drift underneath your feet across two states.

Iruppu Foothills, Waterfall Trail & Narimale Camp

A dramatic ascent starting from the holy foothills of Iruppu, passing through roaring waters and entering the pristine moist evergreen forests of the wildlife sanctuary.

Trek Checklist & Essentials Guide

Gear, leech protection, footwear, and sanctuary regulations

👞 Footwear & Clothing

  • Sturdy trekking shoes with aggressive lug grip
  • Leech socks (mandatory post-monsoon from July to November)
  • 2 quick-dry moisture-wicking full-sleeve shirts
  • 2 breathable trekking pants / track trousers (avoid denim)
  • Light fleece jacket or windcheater for camp evenings

🍴 Hydration & Sustenance

  • 2 reusable water bottles (min 2 litres capacity)
  • Electrolyte / ORS sachets for continuous hydration
  • High-calorie energy bars, dry fruits, and nuts for the trail
  • Personal steel mug and tiffin for eco-friendly camp meals

🛡️ Protection & Medical

  • Headlamp or high-beam torch with extra batteries
  • Waterproof rain poncho or lightweight rain jacket
  • Sun cap, UV sunglasses, and eco-friendly sunscreen
  • Personal medication and anti-histamines if required

👔 Eco Sanctuary Ethics

  • 100% Zero-Plastic policy strictly enforced by Forest Dept
  • No music speakers or loud noise inside the sanctuary
  • Government issued original Photo ID for forest checkposts
  • Leave No Trace – all packaging must be carried back

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details on permits, difficulty, leeches, and season for the Brahmagiri Trek

When is the best season to trek Brahmagiri Peak?

+

The prime season is from October to March when the Western Ghats are bathed in vibrant emerald greens, streams flow abundantly, and clear blue skies provide dramatic cloud-inversion views at sunrise. April to May is warmer but dry, while peak monsoon months (June to September) experience heavy downpours with dense leeches and restricted forest permits.

Are leeches present on the trail, and how should I prepare?

+

Yes, especially in the dense evergreen Shola forest stretch near Iruppu between July and November. We strongly recommend wearing high-quality Leech Socks, tightly tucked trousers, and carrying salt or snuff powder. Once you cross Narimale and reach the open grassy ridge, leeches are virtually absent.

Do we need special government permits for Brahmagiri?

+

Yes. Brahmagiri is an ecologically sensitive Wildlife Sanctuary governed by the Karnataka Forest Department. Entering without an authorized permit and registered forest guide is illegal. When you book with Bootpaths, all official permits, entry fees, and mandatory forest guide escorts are fully arranged in advance for your group.

What is the physical fitness level required?

+

The trek is rated Moderate. You will cover approximately 18 km round-trip over two days with a continuous ascent of ~650m on Day 1. Anyone with basic cardiovascular stamina (capable of jogging 3–4 km or brisk walking for an hour without exhaustion) can comfortably complete this trek.

What are the accommodation facilities at Narimale Camp?

+

Narimale is a rustic, eco-friendly forest camp operated by the Forest Department. Accommodation is in basic shared forest dorm rooms or camping tents with sleeping mats. There is running natural spring water and basic Indian-style washrooms. Please note there is no commercial electricity or cellular network, allowing a true, peaceful digital detox in pristine nature.

How do I reach the base point at Iruppu / Srimangala?

+

The trek base is Srimangala / Iruppu in South Coorg. It is situated ~250 km from Bangalore (5.5 hrs drive), 110 km from Mysore (2.5 hrs), and 95 km from Kannur International Airport. KSRTC buses run regularly to Gonikoppal / Kutta, from where local taxis or autos easily connect to the Iruppu base camp.
