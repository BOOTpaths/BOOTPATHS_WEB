Crown Jewel of Munnar

The Solitary Cloud
Sentinel

Standing majestically isolated between the high ranges of Eravikulam and the deep tropical depression of Bison Valley, Chokkramudi Peak (2,180m / 7,152 ft) is South India's most dramatic single-day high-altitude trek.

Unlike conventional summit trails, Chokkramudi transitions through four distinct ecological biomes in under five hours: beginning in dew-draped emerald tea slopes, plunging into prehistoric moss-carpeted Shola forests, climbing through windswept montane grasslands, and culminating across a thrilling weathered granite knife-edge ridge.

At the summit pinnacle, gaze down at an ocean of rolling white cloud inversions below your boots, while taking in 360-degree vistas stretching from Anamudi (the highest peak in South India) all the way to the blue waters of the Idukki Arch Dam catchment basin. Guided by Bootpaths' certified wilderness navigators, every step is safe, respectful of nature, and purely unforgettable.

Detailed Stage Guide

Deep-dive into trail terrain, wildlife landmarks, and summit navigation

Stage 1

Dawn Gathering at Lockhart Tea Gate (1,620 m)

⌛
Duration: 45 mins
📈
Altitude Gain: 1,620 m → 1,740 m (120 m gain)
🥾
Difficulty & Terrain: Easy. Gentle estate pathway through emerald tea rows.
🌿
Highlights: Crisp mountain dawn, dewy tea aroma, safety briefing, trekking poles distribution.

Meet your Bootpaths mountain guides at the picturesque Lockhart Gap junction as the first twilight purples the eastern sky. Following a detailed safety briefing, headlamp checks, and trek baton handovers, we commence on a gentle winding contour through manicured tea garden tracks. The cool mountain breeze carrying notes of fresh eucalyptus and tea leaves prepares your lungs for the climb.

Lockhart Gap tea slopes waking up in the morning mist

Stage 2

Entering the Ancient Shola Forest Basin (1,740 m)

⌛
Duration: 45 mins
📈
Altitude Gain: 1,740 m → 1,880 m (140 m gain)
🥾
Difficulty: Moderate. Tree roots, mossy boulders, natural stone steps.
🦜
Ecology: Montane wet temperate forest, endemic lichens, babbling mountain brook.

The tea bushes abruptly yield to the mysterious gloom of a virgin Shola forest patch. These stunted ancient trees, draped in thick emerald mosses, hanging lichens, and epiphytic wild orchids, act as giant natural sponges storing Munnar's rainwater. We cross a crystal-clear spring stream where the whistling notes of the Malabar Thrush resonate through the mist.

Gnarled moss-draped branches in the Shola forest canopy
Sunlight piercing the morning forest canopy
Stage 3

High-Altitude Montane Grasslands (1,880 m)

⌛
Duration: 40 mins
📈
Altitude Gain: 1,880 m → 1,990 m (110 m gain)
🥾
Difficulty: Moderate continuous incline over grassy ridges.
🌸
Flora: Strobilanthes kunthiana (Neelakurinji) shrublands & wild mountain grasses.

Emerging from the forest onto the open mountain shoulder, the entire horizon opens up. You are greeted by a breathtaking temperature inversion: below your feet lies a thick, billowing blanket of white clouds, completely concealing the valleys. Here, clusters of the famous Neelakurinji bushes flank the trail among golden grasses and weathered basalt stones.

Stage 4

Kurisumala Ridge & Nilgiri Tahr Sanctuary (1,990 m)

⌛
Duration: 45 mins
📈
Altitude Gain: 1,990 m → 2,110 m (120 m gain)
🥾
Difficulty: Challenging. Granite slab scrambling with guide assistance.
🐐
Fauna: Resident herds of endangered Nilgiri Tahr mountain goats.

We arrive at the scenic Kurisumala rocky plateau, crowned with a heritage stone cross. The vegetation thins into bare volcanic granite. This is prime Nilgiri Tahr habitat — with a quiet approach, our trekkers frequently photograph these majestic horned ibex grazing fearless on precipitous vertical drop-offs. The cool wind accelerates as we navigate the knife-edge crest.

Majestic Nilgiri Tahr standing watch over the valley

Stage 5

The Summit Pinnacle of Chokkramudi (2,180 m / 7,152 ft)

⌛
Duration: 45 mins on peak
📈
Final Altitude: 2,180 m (7,152 ft) • Zenith Milestone
🏆
Achievement: Munnar's Highest Accessible Single-Day Summit!
🔭
360° Sightlines: Anamudi, Mattupetty Lake, Idukki Arch Dam basin, Bison Valley.

One final push up the rocky pinnacle brings you to the summit cairn of Chokkramudi Peak. Standing at 2,180 meters with the Western Ghats laid out at your feet, the sense of liberation is absolute. The cold breeze, warm morning sunlight, and endless vistas of blue mountain ridges stretching into the horizon make every drop of sweat worthwhile. We enjoy dry fruits, hot herbal tea, and celebratory summit portraits.

At the basecamp pavilion, enjoy a piping-hot Kerala breakfast featuring fluffy appams with vegetable stew, fresh fruit, and fresh single-estate Munnar tea, followed by the official Bootpaths trek certificate presentation.

Everything you need to know to prepare for the Chokkramudi summit trek

Chokkramudi is classified as a moderate trek. No prior technical mountaineering experience is required, but reasonable cardiovascular fitness is essential. The route involves approximately 4.5 to 5 hours of walking with continuous ascent through forest trails, grassy hills, and some granite scrambling near Kurisumala. Beginners with active lifestyles tackle it comfortably!

The prime window is September to March. October to January offers the most spectacular sunrise temperature inversions (sea of clouds below the peak) and crisp, cool morning temperatures (12°C–18°C). The monsoon months (June–August) bring lush greenery, mist, and rushing brooks, but rocks can become slippery.

An early morning departure from Lockhart Gap allows you to witness the golden sunrise breaking over the sea of clouds from the upper grassland ridges. Furthermore, by ascending early, you avoid the humid midday mountain sun and beat the afternoon cloud cover that frequently envelopes the peak after 11:00 AM.

Chokkramudi's higher ridges are famous for sightings of the endangered Nilgiri Tahr (mountain goat), wild hares, and vibrant bird species like the Nilgiri Pipit. Elephants generally stay in lower valley corridors. During the post-monsoon months, wet Shola forest patches can have leeches — Bootpaths guides provide organic salt sprays, leech gaiter advice, and safety checks to ensure zero discomfort.

Wear sturdy hiking shoes or trail runners with deep lugged rubber soles — avoid flat running sneakers or smooth slippers. Dress in breathable athletic layers (moisture-wicking t-shirt with a light fleece or windbreaker jacket for the breezy summit). Bring sunglasses, a suncap, sunscreen, and a 1.5-litre reusable water bottle.

Children aged 9 years and above with good stamina regularly complete Chokkramudi. Active seniors accustomed to brisk walking and gentle hill climbs enjoy the route up to the Kurisumala Plateau (1,990m). For families, our guides pace the group gently with regular breathing and hydration pauses.

The trek starts near Lockhart Gap / Gap Road viewpoint along the Kochi–Dhanushkodi National Highway (NH 85), approximately 14 km (25-minute drive) from Munnar town. We provide exact Google Maps pins upon booking, and round-trip jeep/cab pick-ups from your hotel can be arranged seamlessly on request.
