About the Trek

Kerala's Premier
Conservation Ridge Trek

Spanning 925 square kilometers of untamed Western Ghats wilderness, the Periyar Tiger Reserve in Idukki is renowned worldwide as a sanctuary where wildlife roams without boundaries.

The Periyar Border Hiking program is an extraordinary, conservation-oriented wilderness trek along the boundary zone of this legendary reserve. Ascending from 900 meters to high ridges reaching over 1,300 meters, this trail offers an unmatched dual perspective: sweeping views of dense Kerala rainforest valleys on one side and the sprawling plains of Tamil Nadu on the other.

Accompanied by two armed Kerala Forest Department guards and indigenous tribal trackers from the Mannan and Paliyan communities, you will traverse remote shola-grasslands, elephant migration corridors, and ancient deciduous trails while actively participating in tiger reserve boundary monitoring.

Essential Gear & Packing Checklist

👞 Footwear & Trail Protection

  • Ankle-support trekking boots with rugged grip soles
  • Anti-leech cotton socks / gaiters (mandatory during monsoon)

👕 Apparel & Color Code

📸 Optics, IDs & Essentials

  • Original Government Photo ID card (Aadhaar / Passport)
  • Compact binoculars (8x42 or 10x42 recommended)
  • Personal medications and insect repellent balm
🛡️

Forest Department Rules & Safety Code

Crucial eco-regulations, group discipline, and wildlife safety protocols

🚫 Strict Tiger Reserve Mandates

  • Zero-Plastic Zone: Single-use plastic bottles, snack wrappers, and polythene are strictly prohibited inside PTR.
  • Escort Discipline: All trekkers must strictly remain between the lead armed guard and rear tracker at all times.
  • Wildlife Distance: Maintain absolute silence and a minimum 50-meter safety clearance when encountering elephants or bison.
  • No Smoking or Alcohol: Strictly banned under the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding the Periyar Border Hiking trek, permits, and fitness

What is the physical fitness level required for Border Hiking?

The Periyar Border Hiking trek covers 15 to 18 km with moderate to steep ascents up to 1,300m altitude. A good level of basic physical stamina and walking endurance is required. It is suitable for regular walkers, active fitness enthusiasts, and seasoned trekkers. Minimum age is 12 years.

Is this trek safe regarding wild animals like Elephants and Tigers?

Yes, exceptionally safe. Every hiking group is mandatorily escorted by 2 armed Kerala Forest Department guards and indigenous tribal trackers with generations of deep forest experience. They know animal movement corridors and ensure safe observation distances.

What should I wear and bring on the day of the trek?

Wear muted earth-toned clothes (dark greens, brown, khaki). Sturdy trekking shoes with ankle grip are essential. Carry a reusable water bottle, poncho/raincoat, sunglasses, and your government photo ID for permit verification. Single-use plastic bottles and polythene bags are strictly prohibited.

How far in advance should I book my slot?

Because the Forest Department strictly restricts the trek to just two small squads (max 6 trekkers per squad) per day to preserve the delicate ecosystem, we recommend reserving your slots at least 7 to 15 days in advance, especially for weekends and peak season (Oct–May).
