About the Trail

Arippa Forest Trek & Tribal Camp

Arippa lies in the Western Ghats foothills, about 52 km from Thiruvananthapuram city. The first afternoon is a short guided walk under evergreen cover and along streamside paths, with a visit to a nearby Indigenous settlement when local hosts are available. The longer walk the next morning takes in Vaidoorya Kunnu, the Myristica freshwater swamp, and the Muppathadi palm stop.

The swamp is the feature to slow down for—look for the stilt roots that help the wild nutmeg relatives stand upright in waterlogged soil. Arippa is also known for birdwatching, so the morning walk includes time to listen and scan the canopy before continuing to the hill and wetland sections.

Detailed Day-by-Day Schedule

Comprehensive step-by-step breakdown of the Arippa 2-day expedition

▾
Day 1

Thiruvananthapuram to Arippa & 4 km Forest Walk

▸

1:00 PM Pickup: Report at Thiruvananthapuram Central, Thampanoor (please have lunch before reporting). We drive about 52 km through scenic countryside towards the Western Ghats foothills.

2:30 PM Check-In & Guide Meetup: Arrive at the Arippa Ecotourism Village, settle into your forest accommodation, and meet your certified local guide.

3:30 PM 4 km Nature Walk: Embark on an easy 4 km guided trail beneath lush evergreen canopies and along quiet streamside paths. If the local hosts are available, visit a nearby Indigenous settlement to learn about traditional forest living (always ask before photographing people or homes).

5:30 PM Evening Tea & Snacks: Return to the base camp to freshen up and enjoy hot tea and local snacks.

6:00 PM Storytelling & Campfire: Gather around for community storytelling and trail tales; a campfire will be lit if weather conditions and forest site rules permit.

8:00 PM Dinner & Rest: Enjoy a wholesome Kerala dinner and spend the night in a forest hut or cottage immersed in nocturnal wilderness sounds.

Day 2

Vaidoorya Kunnu & Myristica Swamp (10 km Forest Trek)

▸

6:30 AM Wake-Up & Birding: Rise early to the melodious morning chorus of forest birds echoing across the camp site.

7:30 AM Camp Breakfast: Fuel up with a nourishing Kerala breakfast before setting out on the trail.

8:00 AM 10 km Guided Circuit: Begin the main 10 km wilderness trek. Climb the uphill stretch of Vaidoorya Kunnu for sweeping panoramic viewpoints above the tree cover. Traverse ancient wetlands to inspect the fascinating stilt roots of the prehistoric Myristica swamp, and pause at the Muppathadi palm stop. Enjoy an optional, refreshing stream dip where permitted and when water levels are safe.

12:30 PM Return to Camp: Head back to camp to freshen up and pack your belongings.

1:30 PM Wholesome Lunch: Savor a freshly prepared camp lunch.

2:00 PM Departure: Check out from Arippa and board return transport heading back to Thiruvananthapuram.

~6:00 PM Drop-Off: Arrive back at Thiruvananthapuram Central Railway Station (please allow extra buffer time when booking onward travel connections).

Forest Safety & Eco-Ethics

Zero-plastic mandate, community etiquette, and safety protocols

▾
  • ● Zero Plastic & Leave No Trace: Single-use plastic bottles, wrappers, and non-biodegradable items are strictly prohibited in the forest reserve. Carry all wrappers back out with you.
  • ● Indigenous Community Etiquette: Follow the hosts' lead on conversations and photography. Always ask before photographing people or homes.
  • ● Stream Dipping Safety: Bathing in the stream is optional and goes ahead only where forest staff permit it and the local guide confirms suitable water conditions. During heavy rain or high flow, the group skips water entry.
  • ● Quiet Forest Birding: Walk quietly with the guide and pause when you hear movement or calls in the canopy. Earth-tone clothing is recommended.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Arippa Forest Trek & Tribal Camp expedition

Is Arippa suitable for first-time trekkers?

+

Yes, if you're comfortable walking about 10 km on Day 2. The first afternoon is a shorter 4 km walk. The second day adds an uphill section and potentially muddy or slippery footing.

Where will we sleep?

+

The itinerary uses a forest hut or cottage for the overnight stay. Contact BOOTpaths for exact room-sharing and washroom arrangements for your departure.

Can we bathe in the stream?

+

A dip is optional and goes ahead only where forest staff permit it and the local guide confirms suitable conditions. During heavy rain or high flow, the group skips water entry.

What happens during the community visit?

+

The local guide introduces the group if the hosts are available. Follow the hosts' lead on conversations, photography, and which spaces visitors may enter.

Do I need a separate forest permit?

+

Arippa trekking requires Forest Department permission. The package includes forest entry arrangements for the guided walks. Carry a valid government-issued photo ID for check-in.

What should I pack for Arippa?

+

Bring grippy trekking shoes, a poncho or rain jacket, dry clothes for camp, spare socks, a headlamp, a reusable water bottle, and a dry pouch for your phone. Binoculars are handy for the morning birdwatching walk. Carry all wrappers back out with you.
