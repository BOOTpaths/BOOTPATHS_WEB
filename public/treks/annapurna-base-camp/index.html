About the Trail

Annapurna Base Camp
10-Day Journey

The first steps take you up to Ghandruk, where stone houses sit above terraced fields and the outline of Machhapuchhre rises beyond the rooftops. Over the next few days, you'll descend to river crossings, climb the stone stairways of Chhomrong and follow the Modi Khola valley through bamboo forest.

By Deurali, the landscape opens up and you can see how far the trail has brought you. The approach to Annapurna Base Camp is a day of two base camps. At Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700 m), you'll look up at the distinctive summit known as Fishtail, before walking farther into the Annapurna Sanctuary where Annapurna I, Annapurna South, Hiunchuli and Machhapuchhre rise around a glacial basin at 4,130 m.

Why Trek to Annapurna Base Camp?

Key moments and natural perspectives along the Modi Khola into the Annapurna Sanctuary

01

A ring of snow-covered Himalayan peaks

Look from Annapurna I (8,091 m) towards Annapurna South, Hiunchuli and Machhapuchhre inside the Sanctuary.

02

Two base camps in one day

Pass through Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700 m) on your climb into the glacial basin of ABC (4,130 m).

03

A night at Annapurna Base Camp

Experience the Sanctuary in evening light and step out at dawn to watch first light hit the snow walls.

04

Gurung village life in Ghandruk

Spend your first trail night among traditional stone houses, terraced fields, and mountain views.

05

Stone stairways & river crossings

Traverse hillside settlements, river crossings, and the signature stone stairways of the Modi Khola route.

Quick Itinerary (Overview)

10-day schedule overview with walking hours, travel segments & overnight halts

🧭 Official Expedition Trail Route

Annapurna Base Camp Sanctuary Route

Gorakhpur ➜ Pokhara (820 m) ➜ Kimche ➜ Ghandruk (1,940 m) ➜ Chhomrong (2,170 m) ➜ Bamboo (2,310 m) ➜ Deurali (3,200 m) ➜ MBC (3,700 m) ➜ Annapurna Base Camp (4,130 m) ➜ Bamboo ➜ Jhinu Danda ➜ Pokhara ➜ Gorakhpur

DAY 1

Gorakhpur → Pokhara (820 m)

Overnight: Pokhara | Report in Gorakhpur at 11:00 am IST. Cross-border road drive to Pokhara (allow 10+ hours).
DAY 2

Pokhara → Kimche (1,600 m) → Ghandruk (1,940 m)

Walking: ~1 hr | Overnight: Ghandruk teahouse | Drive to Kimche, walk up to Ghandruk village. Look for Annapurna South & Machhapuchhre.
DAY 3

Ghandruk → Komrong Danda (2,200 m) → Chhomrong (2,170 m)

Walking: ~5 hrs | Overnight: Chhomrong teahouse | Follow stone paths through Komrong, descend valley and climb stairways to Chhomrong.
DAY 4

Chhomrong → Sinuwa (2,350 m) → Bamboo (2,310 m)

Walking: 4–5 hrs | Overnight: Bamboo teahouse | Descend towards river, climb to Sinuwa, continue through cooler woodland to Bamboo.
DAY 5

Bamboo → Dovan (2,500 m) → Himalaya (2,900 m) → Deurali (3,200 m)

Walking: 5–6 hrs | Overnight: Deurali teahouse | Follow upper valley as forest thins and valley walls close in towards Deurali.
DAY 6

Deurali → Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3,700 m) → Annapurna Base Camp (4,130 m)

Walking: 5–6 hrs | Overnight: Annapurna Base Camp teahouse | Climb past MBC into the Annapurna Sanctuary and spend the night at ABC.
DAY 7

Annapurna Base Camp → MBC → Bamboo (2,310 m)

Walking: 6–7 hrs | Overnight: Bamboo teahouse | Step out for sunrise, then retrace upper valley via Deurali on longest sustained descent day.
DAY 8

Bamboo → Chhomrong (2,170 m) → Jhinu Danda (1,780 m) → Pokhara (820 m)

Walking: 5–6 hrs + detour | Overnight: Pokhara | Return via Chhomrong, optional hot springs visit, then drive 3–4 hrs to Pokhara.
DAY 9

Pokhara (820 m) → Gorakhpur

Overnight: Gorakhpur (late arrival) | Morning in Pokhara, leave by road in afternoon; expected arrival around 10–11 pm IST.
DAY 10

Gorakhpur → Hometown

Schedule onward train or flight for Day 10 (preferably 2nd half of Day 10 or Day 11 buffer).
