Munnar's Last Frontier

Untamed Mountain Ridges &
Timeless Shola Wilderness

Perched right on the dramatic border of Kerala and Tamil Nadu, Yellapatty (Ellapetty) is famously revered as the "Last Village of Munnar."

Situated roughly 30 km past Munnar town along the scenic Mattupetty-Kundala highway, this hidden gem leaves tourist crowds behind. Here, rolling emerald tea estates terminate into pristine montane shola forest corridors and precipitous rocky ridges that drop dramatically into the Tamil Nadu plains.

Trekkers ascend along century-old British plantation paths, cross fresh mountain streams, and set up camp on cliff edges where the famous "Cloud Bed" phenomenon unfolds at twilight and dawn — presenting a mesmerizing ocean of white clouds stretching into the horizon.

Need to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your Yellapatty high-altitude adventure.

Is the Yellapatty trek suitable for beginners and families?
Yes, absolutely! The Yellapatty trek is rated Easy to Moderate. The ascent covers approximately 4.5 km on Day 1 along well-graded tea paths and forest tracks. Hikers with basic general fitness, teenagers, and active families can comfortably complete this trail without prior mountaineering experience.
When is the best time to witness the Sea of Clouds (Cloud Beds)?
The "Cloud Bed" phenomenon is most consistent between October and March, as the winter atmospheric temperature inversions create dense white cloud sheets trapped beneath the 2,250m peaks between 05:45 AM and 07:30 AM. Post-monsoon months (September–November) offer the lush greenest landscapes.
What are the toilet and washroom arrangements at the campsite?
The campsite features dedicated, sanitized dry eco-toilets and basic partitioned western/eastern washrooms with running water. While it is an authentic off-grid mountain campsite, cleanliness and privacy are strictly maintained.
How cold does it get at night, and what warm gear is provided?
Night temperatures at the ridge generally range from 10°C to 14°C, occasionally dipping to 6–8°C in peak winter (December–January). Bootpaths provides high-grade sleeping bags and foam mattresses inside sealed windproof tents. We advise bringing a fleece jacket, woolen cap, and warm socks.
Is there mobile phone network connectivity at Yellapatty?
Mobile network coverage (BSNL and occasionally Airtel/Jio) is available at Yellapatty village base. At the high ridge campsite, connectivity is patchy to non-existent. We recommend informing your loved ones of your schedule prior to ascending the ridge on Day 1.
How do I reach Yellapatty from Munnar town?
Yellapatty is situated approximately 28–32 km east of Munnar on the Top Station Road. You can reach it via private taxi, auto-rickshaw, or KSRTC buses operating along the Munnar–Top Station route (passing Mattupetty Dam, Echo Point, and Kundala). Bootpaths can also assist in arranging taxi pick-up from Munnar town.
